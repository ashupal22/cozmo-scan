# Benchmark report

Generated by `bench/benchmark_report.py` from `bench/results/`. Methods and discussion are in [`bench/README.md`](../bench/README.md), gate definitions in [`docs/gates.md`](gates.md).

**What the benchmark set is.**
- Our three Stray Scanner walks of one flat (`c7d28f72c6` and `1a8384c3f6` cover the whole flat, `c00a170fe1` part of it). They are the input for all three tiers: the LiDAR data itself, its RGB stream as the video, and stills cut from it as per-room photo folders.
- ARKitScenes: six walks in two venues with laser ground truth, for LiDAR floor and ceiling.
- HouseLayout3D: 23 real buildings cut into rooms, for the stitch solver.

**What is missing.** We had no tape or laser measurements of our own rooms, no staged damage, and no consumer-app capture. Video and photo accuracy is therefore measured against our LiDAR plans of the same walks, not against tape.

**Runtime.** LiDAR and photo runs are live and take seconds to a few minutes. A cold video run takes about 10 s per second of video (378 s for a 37 s clip), so a 3-minute walkthrough would need about 30 minutes, over A-RUNTIME (10 min). **Determinism:** cached runs replay exactly; a fresh DA3 run on Apple's GPU is not bit-identical, and the cold run above gave 2 rooms and 20.32 m² against 3 rooms and 19.82 m² from the cache.
